What is the statutory timeline for an Income Certificate? ▼
Under the RTS Act, an Income Certificate must be issued within 14 working days after scrutiny by the Talathi and Tehsildar.
Which documents are required? ▼
Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, Address Proof (electricity bill/tax receipt), and Income Self-Declaration.
How does Sarthi auto-fill work? ▼
Sarthi extracts your verified identity, address, and income details from the 4 uploaded documents and fills Form 1-A in 3 seconds.
